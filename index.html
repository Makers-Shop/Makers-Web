Who We Are

A maker-first company built by engineers

We started out solving a simple problem — sourcing components that simply weren't available in Malaysia. That mission grew into something much bigger.

Makers Shop was established in 2014 to serve local education institutions, R&D centres, and hobbyists who needed sensors, training kits and components that were difficult to source locally. As demand grew, we moved into distributorship with key suppliers, allowing us to offer a far wider range of products. We are operating at Penang, Malaysia.

As the sole distributor for the Arduino Microcontroller Prototyping Board, we focus on keeping our entire product ecosystem Arduino-compatible — from 3D printers to CNC machines, all controlled with Arduino-based processing.

Excellent customer service and quality products remain the core value of our business. We monitor the industry closely and continuously upgrade our web store to improve the shopping experience.
